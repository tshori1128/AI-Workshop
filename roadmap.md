# Roadmap

## What this is
A study task list for people learning a language. Each task is tagged with a skill, so a learner can see which skill they have been avoiding.

## What Done means
A stranger opens the live site, signs up with an email and password, and adds a few study tasks, each tagged with one of six fixed skills: Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, Vocabulary, Grammar. They tick some off as done and see a count of finished tasks per skill, with the skill or skills they have finished least labeled "Avoiding." They log out, come back later, log in, and find everything as they left it. That is the whole project; anything beyond it is Backlog.

## Slices
1. Sign up and log in | done-criteria: (a) On the live site, a visitor with no account clicks "Sign up," enters a new email and a password, and lands on a page showing that email and a "Log out" button. (b) They click "Log out" and see the "Log in" button again; typing the tasks page address into the browser sends them to the login page. (c) They try to log in with the right email and a wrong password, see an error message, and stay logged out. (d) After logging in, they close the tab, reopen the site, and still see their email and "Log out." | status: ACTIVE
2. Tasks that stay | done-criteria: (a) A logged-in person types "Watch one episode without subtitles," picks "Listening" from a skill dropdown, clicks "Add," and sees the task in their list labeled "Listening." (b) They log out and log back in, and the same task is still there with the same label. (c) Logged in as a second test account, they do not see the first account's tasks. (d) They tick a task's checkbox, see it marked done, refresh the page, and it is still marked done. | status: pending
3. Skill balance | done-criteria: (a) On a fresh account, a person adds and ticks off two Reading tasks and one Speaking task, and the page shows Reading 2, Speaking 1, and 0 for the other four skills. (b) In that same state, the four skills at 0 are labeled "Avoiding," and Reading and Speaking are not. (c) They tick off one Listening task, refresh, and Listening shows 1 and is no longer labeled "Avoiding." (d) They untick one Reading task, refresh, and Reading shows 1. | status: pending
